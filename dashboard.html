MAKE IT WORK FOR YOU

Choose your pauses.

Keep the reminders that help. Adjust the ones that get in the way.

New tabs

Give yourself a little space between opening tabs. Extra destinations are saved on a pause page.

Set 0 minutes to observe without starting a tab cooldown.

Mouse reminders

A quiet cue when movement or clicks have no clear action. Repeated signals can prompt a pause.

Following links

Notice when one link leads to another within 20 minutes. Reload and Back do not deepen the trail.
