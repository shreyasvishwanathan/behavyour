# Behavyour - A different kind of self improvement extension

Although this extension is heavily AI built, the design and analytics for this extension has been fully ideated through personal human pain. It works on Chromium based browsers - for now.

## Philosophy and Intent

We have shorts, social media, infi ai content, infinite sensational content and all ungodly things on the web now. I reside in India, where people are increasingly consuming without restraint.

I tried all types of "well-being" apps and extensions, and realized that they do not help with the reconditioning of the mind at the core. 

### The major issue is - **ALL OF THE ATTEMPTS OF THESE "WELL-BEING" APPS \newline OCCUR WHEN OUR MIND IS "MADE UP" TO CONSUME - WHEN THE IMPULSE IS STRONG ENOUGH TO BE IMPOSSIBLE TO STOP!!**

Let's face it, we are human beings - no matter how smart - who have a limited energy to spend on the highly demanding conscious behaviour. **The best way to rewire is not when the impulse is unstoppable, but before it starts to become!**

So, what is the easiest way to correct before the impulse is unstoppable? We start at mouse pattern - useless clicks, roaming, etc. Then we go to the tab opening and hyperlink click patterns. We have a personal dashboard which will help you analyze the behaviour in your sessions.


## Path Ahead

1. Make this app more human - i.e., contributors fully humanly parse the source code. This is important to encode the shared pain correctly in its design.

2. Make the dashboard more interpretable, so people get to understand themselves and their behaviour more deeply.


I encourage and request everyone to please use this and suggest ideas/improvements!
